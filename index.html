Is CollageMill free to use?

Yes. Every style, transform and export option is free, with no account or watermark. Everything runs in your browser.

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

No. Photos are processed locally with the Canvas API and never leave your device. The page even keeps working offline once loaded.

How many photos can I arrange?

From 1 to 25. Optimized one-click layouts cover 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 9, 12, 14, 16, 20, 24 and 25 photos, and empty slots can be filled with demo photos.

What formats and sizes can I export?

PNG (with transparent background support) and JPG at 1x, 2x or 3x resolution — up to about 4800 px on the long edge.

What do the Tilt, Bend and Cube transforms do?

They lay your grid out in 3D: Tilt leans the whole wall into a perspective plane, Bend wraps it around an invisible cylinder like a curved gallery wall, and Cube folds photos onto the faces of a 3D box.
